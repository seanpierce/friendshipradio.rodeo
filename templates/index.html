FRIENDSHIP RADIO RODEO

About

Friendship Radio Rodeo is going to be an online radio station. Folks will be streaming live and sending in pre-records from all over the world.

For the time being, you will be listening to weird experiments admin is implementing in order to work out the kinks OR whatever our older brother, Intro To Rhythm is sending out into the world.

Another project of mine is FLYER ESCAPE - a source for live music and other community events in Oregon in the form of a website.

Check out Flyer Mire and Pants on Flyers.

While I am at it - I will plug one more member of this family of underground things: Free Spirit News "A harbinger of ancient truths and modern lies."


Friendly Note: If you'd like to support this project, donations are welcome but never required! Community matters.
